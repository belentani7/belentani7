## Philosophy

### Why Zero-Token Routing?

LLMs are expensive. Most tasks don't need them. **MetaSkill** proves that intelligent routing can classify 16 task archetypes with zero token burn — saving money, reducing latency, and proving that sometimes the best AI is the AI you don't call.

### Why Social Impact?

Technology that doesn't serve communities is just a toy. **Cruzando el Charco** and **ManosAbiertas** exist because information should be free, accessible, and confidential for those who need it most.

### Why Creative Technology?

Code is art. Music is math. The intersection is where magic happens. The **DUCK ecosystem** and **The Judas Experience** blur the line between engineering and expression — because why should creativity be constrained by tools that don't understand art?
