UTILITY SURFACE / LOCAL FIRST

Tools that
earn their place.

Pixel-precise glass, practical software and honest limits. Your files stay in your browser. Each utility is designed as a self-contained operation, with no registration or third-party file service.

01 / IMAGING

Batch image converter

Convert up to 20 local images into PNG, JPEG or WebP. Resize with aspect ratio preserved. Download individually or as a standards-compatible ZIP.

＋ Drop images here or choose files 20 files maximum · 20 MB each · 100 MB combined · 24 MP output cap

02 / VIDEO POST-PRODUCTION

Subtitle time studio

Convert SRT/VTT, shift timestamps and export clean text. The tool processes existing captions — it does not invent speech recognition for local video files.

≋
TEXT → TIMED DRAFT

No audio recognition is performed. This creates estimated cue timings from a written script; correct the timings against your actual recording.

03 / PROFESSIONAL COMMUNICATION

CV clarity inspector

A privacy-preserving, heuristic text review: structure, contact presence, readability length and evidence-oriented suggestions. Not an ATS score and not AI-generated hiring advice.

◈
Your text is analyzed in memory when you press Inspect.

Do not paste personal identifiers if you do not want them present in your browser. This page has no CV upload or analytics service.

04 / CREATIVE PRODUCTION

Storyboard architect

Plan and export real shot lists for reels, music videos and creative campaigns: duration, camera, movement, visual intention and sound.

◇
ShotSecondsCameraMovementVisualAudioNotes

05 / ART DIRECTION

Image color sampler

Derive a compact palette from the pixels in your own image. Sampled colors are approximate; export swatches as reusable CSS variables.

◉
Choose a local image to begin.
06 / DOCUMENT PROCESSING

Images into PDF

Combine photographs and screenshots into an A4 PDF without sending them to a server. Every input is rasterized locally as JPEG, removing source metadata. One page per image, with aspect ratio preserved.

07 / AUDIO PROCESSING

Audio to WAV

Decode compatible local audio formats, inspect duration, peak and RMS, then export 16-bit mono or stereo PCM WAV. Decoding happens in the browser's Web Audio engine.

No audio selected. Sample count is limited to protect your device.
08 / VIDEO TOOLS

Video contact sheet

Extract evenly spaced still frames from your own local video and combine them into a labelled PNG contact sheet. Codec support depends on your browser; no media is uploaded.

▦
Choose a video to export timed stills.

EVIDENCE / NOT EMPTY PROMISES

All implemented utilities use native browser APIs and a small, auditable JavaScript core. Results vary with supported codecs, memory limits and browser permissions. No social-platform restrictions are bypassed.
